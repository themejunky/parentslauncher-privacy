Launcher for Parents – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Launcher for Parents (package com.parentslauncher.app) is a home-screen app published by ThemeJunky. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

Data we collect

None. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising SDK and does not connect to any server of ours. It does not request the Internet permission.

Data stored on your device

The app saves only its own settings on your phone: which app is assigned to each button, your appearance preferences, whether the setup assistant was completed, and whether Premium was purchased. This data never leaves the device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Installed apps

To show the "All apps" list and to detect your default camera, gallery, contacts, browser, calendar, settings, phone and messaging apps, the app reads the list of launchable apps on the device. This information is used only on the device and is not collected or transmitted.

Purchases

Premium is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see your payment details. Google's processing of purchases is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Companion apps

The setup assistant may suggest installing our optional apps SMS Plus and Keyboard Plus from Google Play. Each has its own privacy policy. Launcher for Parents only checks whether they are installed in order to open them.

Children

The app is intended for adults and seniors and is not directed at children under 13.

Samsung Galaxy Store and Huawei AppGallery

This section applies to com.parentslauncher.app.samsung on Samsung Galaxy Store and com.parentslauncher.app.huawei on Huawei AppGallery. For these Android editions, it takes precedence over store-specific statements elsewhere on this page.

Purchases. Galaxy Store builds use Samsung In-App Purchase (Samsung Electronics); AppGallery builds use HUAWEI IAP (Huawei/Aspiegel, depending on your region). Google Play Billing is not used. The app receives product IDs, purchase tokens or identifiers, receipts/signatures and purchase status to verify ownership, unlock Premium and restore purchases. The store handles payment details; we do not receive your card details. See the Samsung Privacy Policy and Huawei Privacy Statement and the notices shown by your store. Manage purchases and any subscriptions in the store where you bought them; uninstalling does not cancel subscriptions.

Ads. These editions contain no advertising SDK: no AdMob, UMP, Petal Ads, Liftoff or Meta Audience Network.

Analytics and push. On Galaxy Store and AppGallery builds, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, Performance Monitoring, Cloud Messaging and App Check are not used. The Firebase analytics, crash-reporting and push descriptions elsewhere on this page do not apply to these editions.

Permissions and online services. Unlike the Google Play edition described above, these OEM editions request INTERNET and network-state permissions for native purchasing, ownership queries and direct store receipt checks. Installed-app lists and settings stay on the device. There is no app-operated purchase-verification backend. Opening your contacts or phone app does not give this launcher access to your contacts or call log. Parents Premium is a one-time purchase, with no subscriptions.
